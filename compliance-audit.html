7critical blockers

The prototype covers almost every page in the sitemap (§3.2) and most of the detail, listing and form templates, and the RTL layout mirrors as §7.3 requires. Three things block the core journeys in Figure 7: the tourist cannot reach the entrepreneur (the "contact" route points at the home newsletter anchor), site search returns nothing (§2.1), and pages have no language-specific addresses (§3.1). Five features the report removed or excludes are still present (travel ideas, background music, hotels under "where to stay", favourites, weather). The remaining gaps are content accuracy (programme code, placeholder emails and social links) and the §9 accessibility, SEO and weight rules, which are implementation work for the production build rather than design changes.
